About Treena

A voice called to rebuild hope

"I had a dream to rebuild God's people and be rebuilt by Him."

Treena Wicoff is a faith-inspired singer, songwriter, and podcaster with a mission to bring hope, encouragement, and spiritual renewal to as many hearts as possible. Her journey began with a simple act: picking up the phone and singing to a sick friend. That small moment became a larger calling.

Guided by a deep sense of purpose, Treena creates for people carrying stress, anxiety, loss, and doubt. Through music, Scripture, and honest reflection, she reminds listeners that God is present in the middle of real life.

Her podcast is an extension of that same mission: short, heartfelt episodes designed to fuel your faith and send you back into your day with renewed purpose.
